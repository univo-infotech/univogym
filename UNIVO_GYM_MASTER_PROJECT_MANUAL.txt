1. THE UNIVO VISION & ROLE-BASED ACCESS HIERARCHY
================================================================================
Univo Gym Management commercial fitness clubs, crossfit studios aur academies ke
liye ek complete multi-role operating system hai.

System me 4 role-based portals hain:
1. Owner (Super Admin):
   - Poora financial control: Revenue, Daily/Monthly Profit & Loss, Staff Salary.
   - Plans banana aur pricing set karna.
   - Members edit, delete, gate lock/unlock aur reports dekhna.
2. Trainer (Coach Portal):
   - Sirf unhi members ko dekh sakta hai jo uske under assigned hain.
   - Daily workout plans, customized diet charts aur client transformation photos.
   - Apna shift timing aur PT client roster manage karna.
3. Front Desk / Staff:
   - Walk-in inquiries register karna, admission lena, fee collect karna.
   - Staff attendance mark karna aur equipment stock maintain karna.
   - Sensitive profit/owner reports hidden rehte hain.
4. Member (Client Portal):
   - Member apne phone par apna active plan, expiry date aur daily streak dekh sakta hai.
   - Assigned trainer, daily workout schedule, diet chart aur official fee receipts.

2. INITIAL GYM SETUP (FIRST DAY CONFIGURATION SOP)
================================================================================
Kisi bhi gym me pehle din Univo Gym start karne ke 4 aasan steps:

Step 2.1: Gym Profile & Branding (Settings):
- Business Profile me Gym ka Legal Name, Phone, Email, Address daalein.
- Gym ka Logo aur UPI QR Code upload karein (taaki invoices par auto-print ho).
- Liability waiver aur receipt terms & conditions set karein.

Step 2.2: Membership Plans Create Karein (Memberships):
- Standard plans banayein:
  * 1-Month Basic (₹2,500)
  * 3-Month Pro (₹6,500)
  * 6-Month Transformation (₹11,000)
  * 1-Year Elite (₹18,000)
- Duration (in days/months), pricing aur features set karein.

Step 2.3: Trainers & PT Packages Add Karein (Trainers):
- Trainers ka naam, specialization, phone aur shift timings (Morning, Evening, Both) add karein.
- Har trainer ke personal training packages daalein (e.g. 1 Month PT ₹4,500).

Step 2.4: Add-on Services Configure Karein (Services):
- Gym ke extra services set karein: Steam & Sauna, Personal Locker, Diet Consultation.
- Billing type: Per Month ya One-Time.

3. MEMBER ADMISSION & ONBOARDING (2 SEAMLESS METHODS)
================================================================================

METHOD A: Direct In-Person Admission ("+ Add Member" Button):
1. Member Directory me upar right side me "+ Add Member" par click karein.
2. Form bharein: Name, Phone, Gender, Workout Slot (Morning/Evening/Night).
3. Plan Select Karein: Gym plan select karein, duration aur price auto-fill hoga.
4. Personal Trainer (Optional): Agar member coach chahta hai toh coach select karein.
5. Payment Entry: Paid amount dalein (Full ya Partial Due).
6. Save: "Save Member" dabate hi member Active ho jata hai aur WhatsApp invoice popup aa jata hai.

METHOD B: 10-Minute Self-Registration WhatsApp Link:
- Rush hours me reception par line se bachne ke liye:
1. Upar "Share 10-Min Link" button par click karein.
2. Link member ke WhatsApp par bhej dein.
3. Member apne mobile par apna photo upload karta hai, details bharta hai aur digital waiver sign karta hai.
4. Link 10 minute me auto-expire ho jati hai security ke liye. Member turant directory me add ho jata hai.

4. MEMBER DIRECTORY COMMAND CENTER (7 METRIC TABS & 13 BUTTON ACTIONS)
================================================================================

7 AUTOMATED METRIC FILTER TABS:
1. Fully Paid (Green): Un sabhi members ki list jinki fees paid hai aur expiry date aage hai.
2. Ending Soon (Amber): Members jinka plan agle 3 dino ke andar khatam hone wala hai.
3. Expired (Red): Members jinki validity khatam ho chuki hai. Inka gate access automatic lock ho chuka hai.
4. Due (Red Alert): Members jinka admission token/partial fee par hua tha aur balance baki hai.
5. Partial Fee (Amber): Outstanding payment balance wale members.
6. Gym Left (Slate): Wo members jinhone gym chhod diya. Coach slot free ho chuka hai aur login band ho chuka hai.
7. PT Ended (Purple): Member ka gym plan active hai, lekin personal coach package expire ho chuka hai.

HAR EK BUTTON KA PURA KAAM (13 ACTION TRIGGERS):
1. View (Eye Icon):
   - Member ki 360 Profile kholta hai: Attendance history, payment ledger, medical notes aur progress photos.
2. Extend (Calendar Plus):
   - Bina naya bill banaye member ko complimentary days (+10 days ya custom) deta hai (jaise injury/chhutti ke waqt).
3. Gym Renew (Rotate Ccw):
   - Jab plan expire ho jaye toh naya cycle shuru karta hai: naya plan chunein, fees collect karein aur fresh validity start karein.
4. PT Renew (Sparkles Icon):
   - Personal training package renew karta hai, trainer commission calculate karta hai aur shift schedule renew karta hai.
5. Collect / Remaining (Rupee Icon):
   - Due balance collect karne ka fast window. Baki bachi fees jama karta hai aur balance receipt deta hai.
6. Receipt (Receipt Icon):
   - Official payment bill kholta hai. Professional PDF print nikalta hai aur WhatsApp par 1-click me bhejta hai.
7. Edit (Pencil Icon):
   - Member ka phone number, slot, emergency contact, email aur photo update karta hai.
8. Left (LogOut Icon):
   - Member ko 'Left Gym' mark karta hai. Reason record karta hai, trainer ki shift free karta hai aur member portal login band kar deta hai.
9. Rejoin Gym (Rotate Ccw on Left Members):
   - Chhute hue member ke liye special flow: Nayi membership modal kholta hai, validity AAJ KI DATE se start karta hai aur naya bill banata hai.
10. Delete (Trash Icon):
    - Member ko database aur local memory se permanently remove kar deta hai.
11. + Add PT:
    - Mahine ke beech me kisi floor member ko bina uska gym plan reset kiye Personal Trainer allot karta hai.
12. End PT:
    - Member ka gym chalu rakhte hue uska trainer package khatam karta hai aur coach ki shift release karta hai.
13. Lock / Unlock Gate (Biometric Tab):
    - Kisi member ka biometric gate access disciplinary reasons ke liye 1 click me block ya unblock karta hai.

5. PERSONAL TRAINING (PT) LIFECYCLE & TRAINER SCHEDULE
================================================================================
- Gym is Base Rule: Koi bhi client akela PT nahi le sakta, gym membership mandatory foundation hai.
- Independent Validity: Gym Plan (e.g. 3 Months) aur PT Package (e.g. 1 Month) ki apni alag start aur expiry dates hoti hain.
- Automated Shift Release: Jab member PT end karta hai ya left hota hai, trainer ka specific slot (e.g. Morning 7-8 AM) free ho jata hai taaki naya member assign ho sake.
- Separate Bills: Gym fees aur PT fees ke alag-alag transparent bills bante hain taaki coach commission me 0 confusion rahe.

6. UNIVERSAL BIOMETRIC ATTENDANCE & REAL-TIME 4-POINT GATE LOCK
================================================================================

REAL-TIME 4-POINT GATE SECURITY ENGINE:
Jaise hi koi scanner par thumb/face lagata hai, software 0.1 second me 4 checks karta hai:
1. Expiry Check: Agar expiryDate < today -> DENIED: "Membership Expired". Gate lock rehta hai.
2. Due Balance Check: Agar fees pending hai -> DENIED: "Pending Due Balance". Gate lock rehta hai.
3. Left Check: Agar member gym chhod chuka hai -> DENIED: "Account Terminated". Gate lock rehta hai.
4. Owner Lock Check: Agar owner ne lock kiya hai -> DENIED: "Biometric Access Suspended". Gate lock rehta hai.
- Agar charo check pass hain: LCD screen green hoti hai ("ACCESS GRANTED"), daily attendance mark hoti hai, streak +1 badhti hai aur gate open hota hai!

HARDWARE CONNECTIVITY OPTIONS:
- Cloud Webhook Mode (Realtime / e-SSL): Machine menu me Webhook URL daal do, direct internet se cloud sync. No PC needed.
- Local LAN Mode (Secureye S-B8CB): Same Wi-Fi par machine aur computer connected, local bridge punches sync karta hai.
- USB Pen Drive Mode (Offline Gyms): Pen drive lagayi -> U-Flash -> Download GLog -> Software me 1-click upload!

7. BILLING, MULTI-MODE PAYMENTS & WHATSAPP RECEIPTS
================================================================================
- Payment Modes: Cash, Online UPI (QR Code Scan), Direct Bank Transfer, aur Split Payments (Cash + UPI).
- GST & Non-GST: Gym settings se GST percentage aur HSN code toggle kar sakte hain.
- Auto-Invoice: Har payment par unique Receipt Number (e.g. REC-100234), gym branding aur validity dates ke saath PDF bill banta hai.
- WhatsApp Integration: Ek click me member ke WhatsApp par pre-formatted confirmation message aur bill link chala jata hai.

9. EQUIPMENT INVENTORY, STOCK & EXPENSES
================================================================================
